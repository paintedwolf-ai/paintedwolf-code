## Unmodified copies

Redistribute official source or official binaries unchanged and you may leave the marks that are already there — you are passing on the same thing you received.

## Modified builds

If you change the code, build your own binary, or repackage the product, give it your own name and remove the logos, mascots, and other distinctive branding. Do not present it as official Painted Wolf Code or as endorsed by Painted Wolf LLC.

## Extensions and packs

Extension packs, workflows, agents, rules, and skills you write are **yours** — this policy does not reach them, and nothing here restricts publishing or selling them.

Name them so the relationship is clear and the source is not: **"Acme Deploy for Painted Wolf Code"** is fine, and so is describing a pack as compatible with, built for, or working with Painted Wolf Code. Do not lead with the mark ("Painted Wolf Deploy"), and do not imply Painted Wolf LLC published or endorsed it.

## Themes — limited permission

Painted Wolf LLC grants you limited, non-exclusive, royalty-free permission to create, use, publish, and sell themes that alter the visual identity of the app and, as part of that themed experience, the colors and visibility of the **Painted Wolf Code** identity, so a theme can give the whole product a coherent visual voice.

This permission is limited to the visual results that the documented theme interface permits. Today, a theme may:

- change the app's theme-controlled colors, including the colors inherited by the fixed **Painted Wolf Code** wordmark;
- change the two theme-controlled colors of the fixed logo — its field and its bars; and
- show or hide the fixed logo glyph.

The built-in theme system has no field for logo artwork and no control for redrawing or reshaping the fixed logo, replacing the wordmark, or renaming the product. This permission covers only identity changes that the app itself produces through that theme system.

Installing, enabling, or combining themes and other extensions through the documented extension system does not by itself make the app a "modified build" under this policy. This permission applies to a copy that is otherwise permitted to retain the Painted Wolf marks; it does not let a modified build retain the marks by moving its branding changes into theme data.

The permission covers the marks as rendered inside the themed app and in accurate screenshots or recordings used to identify, demonstrate, or offer that theme. It does not permit using the marks as a theme's own logo, as standalone branding, on merchandise, or in a company name, product name, domain, or social handle.

Name and describe a theme so its author and source are clear. **"Nightshade Theme for Painted Wolf Code"** is fine; do not present a theme as an official release of, or as sponsored or endorsed by, Painted Wolf LLC.

Painted Wolf LLC retains ownership of the marks and the goodwill arising from their use. We may review themed uses for compliance with these conditions and require a noncompliant use to be corrected or discontinued. Permission for a particular theme ends if that theme does not comply with these conditions.

## Describing what you built

Use the word marks in plain text to state something true about origin — "derived from Painted Wolf Code", "works with Painted Wolf Code". Do not use them as your product or company name, in a domain or social handle, or in any way that suggests affiliation, endorsement, or support. Except for the themes permission above, logos and stylized marks need written permission.
