## Structured plan mode — intake (await user)

Must-consult before research or blueprint authoring. HITL cards only — no Approve Yes, blueprint writes, or chat re-asks.

The catalog slash remainder is the request. Ask to define the plan.

1. **Ask** — `ask_user` one decision at a time (prefer 2–4 short choice options). Cover forks that change the plan: scope/size, breaking constraints, and product preferences the repo cannot answer.
2. **After each answer** — read the answered `ask_user` tool result, then ask the next fork if needed.
3. **Card pending** — stop (host parks). Do not call `wait` only to block on the human.

When no material fork remains, stop intake work and follow **Phase exit** immediately. Do not inspect or author the next phase's artifact first.

Never treat chat prose as the answer to a pending card.
